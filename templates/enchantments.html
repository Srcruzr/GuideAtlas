How to Reach the Enchanting Area in Wizard Alchemy

First we must go to Ashen Ruins, which is the second island. You need the flying broom because this island is separate from the main one.

When you are about to arrive you will clearly see the area where you can enchant; it is a purple monument, curiously it is on the same island as the mage boss.

How to Get Enchanted Stone in Wizard Alchemy

This material is essential to enchant all your wands; without it you cannot enchant. You only need one for each change to the wand.

Important Wizard Alchemy Enchantment Tips

  • You can find the enchanted stone in your inventory.
  • You can tell which enchantment you got depending on the color of the glow that is visible to the naked eye.
  • You can enchant as many wands as you want, and you can also view the enchantment in your inventory.
