Beginner's Guide

When starting out in Wizard Alchemy, you should first focus on acquiring basic resources and unlocking abilities.

Avoid spending coins at the beginning, as many important items are purchased with in-game currency. Also, explore all nearby areas to find hidden chests.

In addition, we have a special guide for each of the secrets, chests, and other hidden objects.

First steps

First, you should familiarize yourself with each of the game's interfaces, how to use them, how they work, and what benefits they offer.

  • Bag

    • It's located within your Roblox inventory. Here you can access various important data and items for your character.

    In the bag, you'll find the potions you have available or that you've created, collected materials, equipment, brooms, cauldrons, and special properties.

  • Stats

    • Here you'll find all your character's stats, which you can improve as you level up.

    Keep in mind that for each level you gain one point to increase a single stat.

    There is also the Ascension system, where you can increase your rank depending on your level as a mage. This level increases by using attacks and spells.

    In addition, you will need gold to ascend. The higher your rank, the higher the level and the amount of gold required.

    Finally, there is your character's race. Each race has different stats, and generally, the rarer the race, the better the benefits it offers.

  • Index

    • The index contains everything you've discovered, such as potions, materials, enemies, and other items.

    If you haven't yet discovered an object, it won't appear in the index.

  • Settings

    • Here you can change the graphics settings, controls, utilities, and other game options.

    You can access this section from the gear icon located in the top right corner of the screen.

    In addition, here you'll find one of the most important features: the code system. These codes can give you rewards and very useful benefits within the game.

    We have a special guide with all the available codes.

Starting Missions

Complete the starting missions. You can always repeat them to continue obtaining the same resources. These missions are found with Harryint.

Each of these missions offers different rewards depending on its difficulty. The difficulty changes depending on the enemies you face.

Ingredients for Alchemy

On the map, you will find different types of ingredients needed to create potions. These items have golden glints that indicate that can be collected.

Each ingredient has a distinct magical power that will help create better or worse potions.

Important Map Points

There are several important points on the map that will help during the adventure, with potion creation, buying and selling items, and refining potions.

  • Sales Point

    • At this point you can sell potions and materials. Here's a trick that can give you significant benefits during your adventure.

    It's better to sell potions than materials, since potions generate more coins to buy other items.

  • Shopping Point

    • At this point, you can buy various items that increase your character's stats.

    You can buy wands, wizard hats, robes, and other items. Generally, the higher the price, the better the equipment.

  • Potion Point

    • This is one of the most frequently used points, as here you can craft different types of potions.

    The better the materials, the more powerful the potions you make. Each material has a different magic level.

  • Refining Point

    • At this point, you can refine your potions to obtain much higher stats.

    You must refine three potions of the same type to obtain an S Tier. To obtain an SS Tier you will need four S Tier potions, and finally, to obtain an SSS Tier you will need five SS Tier potions.

Important Tips

  • Don't spend gold at the beginning.
  • Don't sell potions quickly; refine them first.
  • Avoid strong enemies in the early stages.
  • Collect all the materials you can.
