How to Find the Mysterious Shop in Wizard Alchemy

The game has a hidden shop that appears every so often and has many very important resources for the adventure and other very interesting things.

Where can we find the mystery shop in Wizard Alchemy?

This shop is flying, that's why it's not easy to find; a ship appears every so often.

How to redeem codes?

To redeem codes, open the game settings by clicking the gear icon in the top right corner. Then find the codes section and enter the code exactly as it appears here.

On this ship we can find several very interesting materials, we can even find wands exclusive to the vendor.

You have a time limit for all these sales, and you can restart sales as many times as you want.

Important Tips

  • You should always have extra gold to be able to buy things.
  • You should always be on the lookout if you come across the store.
  • Keep in mind what you need so you don't spend money on things you don't need.
