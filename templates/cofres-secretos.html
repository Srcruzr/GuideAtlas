Departure Isle Secrets & Chests Guide

All Secret Chests Locations

Each of the secrets on this island is very beneficial to the adventure, as the chests give a large amount of materials, which help in the creation of potions.

You will find two types of chests: one is the normal chest that drops normal materials, and then we have chests that give us better materials.

  • Chests #1

    The first chest is located behind the spawn point; this will be a chest that can easily go unnoticed.

  • Chests #2

    The second chest is very easy to find; you come across it during the adventure, it's not very well hidden.

  • Chests #3

    The third chest is a little hidden, but if you're a curious person you've probably found it, as it's behind a half-finished building next to the miner.

  • Chests #4

    The fourth chest is a little more hidden than the previous one, as it is located in a secret passage in the same cave where we found the third chest; the passage is next to the chest.

  • Chests #5

    Chest 5 is above the cave where we found the previous two chests; in this area we found a total of 3 chests.

  • Chests #6

    Chest 6 is located behind the camp of the traveler who sells us the cauldron that helps us advance in the adventure.

  • Chests #7

    Chest 7 is located on the right path; you will encounter a camp of enemies, and behind them you will find the chest.

  • Chests #8

    Chest 8 is located in the tree house; it's a tree that's easily visible, it's very large, you can climb it with a broom or by climbing.

  • Chests #9

    Chest 9 is very well hidden; it's behind the whole mountain on the right side of the lighthouse.

  • Chests #10

    Chest 10 is located behind the final boss of the island, behind a structure located in the boss cave.

    This wand is very good, it has very good stats and is aesthetically very beautiful, very useful for adventure.
