Ashen Ruins Secrets & Chests Guide

All Secret Chests Locations

Each of the secrets on this island is very beneficial to the adventure, as the chests give a large amount of materials, which help in the creation of potions.

You will find two types of chests: one is the normal chest that drops normal materials, and then we have chests that give us better materials.

  • Chests #1

    This chest is located up on a hill near the spawn point; this chest is the closest and easiest to find if you're curious.

  • Chests #2

    This chest is located by the main entrance to enter the cave where the boss is located; on the right side there is a bridge with an archer protecting it at the bottom.

  • Chests #3

    This chest is located above where you can buy the hat, after the chest on the bridge, it's on the right side, that's where you'll find it.

  • Chests #4

    It is located directly below where we found the previous one; we will find a lava waterfall and this chest is right next to the hat that we can buy.

  • Chests #5

    This chest is located at the very top of the volcano, right next to the best wand on the map.

  • Chests #6

    This chest is one of the most hidden; it is located behind the volcano inside the final boss's cave.

  • Chests #7

    This chest is on the other side of the island; you enter it through a small cave.

  • Chests #8

    It is located above the water island, where there is a waterfall, and is very visible to the eye.

  • Chests #9 #10

    This is located behind the waterfall; in this area you will find two hidden chests, a very good area to collect materials.

  • Chests #11 #12 #13 #14

    These four chests are located on a small, secluded island; you have to fly with the broom to reach them.

  • Chests #15

    This last chest is the furthest away; it is located on a separate island.

  • Chests #16

    This last chest is the furthest away; it is located on a separate island.

Rod, Hat and Broom in Ashen Ruins

  • Secret Rod

    This wand is located above the volcano; it is the best on the entire map, very powerful and ready for the next world.

    The stats of this wand are high, but the price is very high, but it's worth it.

  • Wizard broom

    The broom is found at the spawn point; it is the specific broom for this world and is very fast with very good stats.

  • Wizard Hat

    This hat is hidden behind the lava waterfall.

    This hat has something different: the damage from burning is very low.
